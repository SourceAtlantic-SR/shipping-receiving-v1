💡 The Observation
Working across different environments, I consistently noticed the same gap: internal clients had very limited visibility into what was happening inside Shipping & Receiving.
❓ Daily Questions I Received
"Did my component arrive for Work Order X?"
"Can you send me a photo of the incoming material?"
"Has this order already been shipped?"
The Problem
What was missing?
👁️
Visibility Gap
Internal clients — engineering, sales, operations — had no way to self-serve status information about their Work Orders or shipments.
📷
Manual Photo Requests
Every photo request — receiving condition, equipment identification, outgoing packaging — was handled manually via phone or email.
🖥️
P21 Limitations
Many S&R workflows were not easily accessible in P21. Real-time hold tracking, photo logs and turnaround data were simply not available.
📊 Beyond Operations
I also wanted a management data view — volume trends, carrier mix, turnaround time, hold analysis — to support decision-making and performance reporting.

System Architecture
Two Environments
🔧
Environment 1
Administrator
  • ✅Create and manage all Incoming Forms and Shipping records
  • 📸Upload and link photos to any record
  • 🚚Manage carrier, transport and tracking information
  • ⏸Open and close hold reasons (QA, PO, Pick-Up)
  • 📊Full access to Dashboard and all reports
👥
Environment 2
Viewers / End Users
  • 🔍Order Lookup — search by WO #, SO #, Material or Incoming #
  • 📦View full status of any incoming or outgoing item
  • 📸Access photos attached to any shipment or receipt
  • 📋See hold reasons, shipping details and tracking numbers
  • 📊Read-only access to the Operations Dashboard
⏱ ~3 min
Access Control
Security & Authentication
  • 🔐Email-based authentication — only accounts with a @sourceatlantic.ca email can register
  • ✉️Invite-only registration — access is granted by sharing a direct link — not publicly listed or advertised
  • 🔑Password-protected sessions — every visit requires login; password reset is available via email
  • ☁️Google Firebase — enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with encrypted data storage and secure connections (HTTPS)
  • 📱Works on any device — browser-based, no installation required, accessible from office, workshop or field
⚠️ Important Note
The app is not hosted on the Source Atlantic corporate portal — it runs independently on a secure web address, outside of IT infrastructure constraints.
✅ Who Can Access
Any Source Atlantic employee with a @sourceatlantic.ca email address who receives the registration link. No IT ticket needed.
🔧 Admin vs Viewer
Admins have full write access. Viewers are read-only — they can look up orders and see photos, but cannot modify any record.
